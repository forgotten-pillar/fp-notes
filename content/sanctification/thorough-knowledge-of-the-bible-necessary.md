> [!bible] [Matthew 4:4 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+4:4&version=kjv)
> 4. But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

> [!bible] [Luke 4:4 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+4:4&version=kjv)
> 4. And Jesus answered him, saying, It is written, That man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.

Jesus quoted from Deuteronomy:

> [!bible] [Deuteronomy 8:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+8:3&version=kjv)
> 3. And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; **that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word  that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live**.

The very point that God said in Deuteronomy is just as Israel was fed daily and **continuously** by manna from God, so are we be fed **continuously** by the word of God.

Jesus defeated Satan's temptations by other verses, which were also from Deuteronomy. See [Deuteronomy 6:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+6.13&version=KJV), [6:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+6.16&version=KJV)
