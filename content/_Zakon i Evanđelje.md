## under the law

> [!bible] [Romans 2:12 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+2:12&version=kjv)
> <sup> **12** </sup>For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned **in the law** shall be judged by the law;

In the law means being with the knowledge of the Law.

> [!bible] [Romans 3:19 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+3:19&version=kjv)
> <sup> **19** </sup>Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are **under the law**: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.

The original (en) should be in and not under. "under the law" or "in law" means to have understanding. Those who are breaking the law are under the law.

> [!bible] [Romans 6:14-15 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+6:14-15&version=kjv)
> <sup> **14** </sup>For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not **under the law**, but under grace.
> <sup> **15** </sup>What then? shall we sin, because we are not **under the law**, but under grace? God forbid.

So if we are not under the law, does that means we can sin?

Under the law (hypo) means be under the curse of the law. In the law (en) means to have knowledge of the law.

> [!bible] [Galatians 3:22-23 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+3:22-23&version=kjv)
> <sup> **22** </sup>But the scripture hath concluded all under sin, that the promise by faith of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.
> <sup> **23** </sup>But before faith came, we were kept **under the law**, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed.
