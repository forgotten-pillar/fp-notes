## The War in Heaven

> [!bible] [Revelation 12:7-9 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+12:7-9&version=kjv)
> <sup>**7**</sup> And there was **war in heaven**: **Michael and his angels** fought against **the dragon**; and **the dragon fought and his angels**,
> <sup>**8**</sup> And prevailed not; neither was their place found any more **in heaven**.
> <sup>**9**</sup> And **the great dragon** was cast out, **that old serpent**, **called the Devil**, **and Satan**, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and **his angels were cast out with him**.

We see that there is some cosmic war going on. We see two groups in enmity against each other: angels lead by [[archangel-michael|Michael]], and angels led by dragon, which is the Satan. The place of the war is in heaven, and Satan lost the war in heaven, and was cast down to the earth, together with his angels. The continuation of the text gives us some insights into the nature of this war.

> [!bible] [Revelation 12:10-12 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+12:10-12&version=kjv)
> <sup>**10**</sup> And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and **the kingdom of our God**, **and the power of his Christ**: for **the accuser of our brethren is cast down**, **which accused them before our God day and night**.
> <sup>**11**</sup> And they overcame him **by the blood of the Lamb**, and by **the word of their testimony**; and they loved not their lives unto the death.
> <sup>**12**</sup> Therefore rejoice, ye heavens, and ye that dwell in them. **Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea**! **for the devil is come down unto you**, having great wrath, **because he knoweth that he hath but a short time**.

This passage gives us a lot of information about this conflict. It is still going on. Since Satan is cast down on this earth, our planet is a battleground. The good thing is, this war has an appointed time to end, and Satan has a short time here. What is his work on this planet?

We read that he is in the business of **deception**. He is very successful here on our planet - "...that old serpent, called the Devil, **and Satan**, **which deceiveth the whole world**". He was not successful in Heaven, he lost his war there, but here, he has more success.

The text gives us insight into another work Satan is doing: "*the **accuser of our brethren** is cast down*, *which accused them before our God day and night*". So we see the nature of the weapons - this is a war of accusations and lies, not force.

> [!tip] The Issue of Deception
> The problem of deception is that we can be deceived, without knowing we are deceived. Furthermore, we can believe that we are not deceived, yet in reality we are deceived. Therefore there must exist an **absolute truth**, which is being contended in this cosmic conflict.

We also see that this conflict involves two distinct kingdoms. On one side stands "*the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ*", and on the other, the kingdom of the dragon. This is not merely a clash of individuals — it is a war between two opposing governments.

But there are other participants in this conflict besides angels. The text says: "*they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.*" Who are these? These are people — those saved by Jesus Christ. He is "the Lamb" referred to here, as John the Baptist testified:

> [!bible] [John 1:29 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1:29&version=kjv)
> <sup>**29**</sup> The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold **the Lamb of God**, which taketh away the sin of the world.

So we see that humanity is drawn into this cosmic war, and that the victory over Satan's deceptions is obtained through Christ's sacrifice.

## What Is the Contention About?

To understand the nature of this conflict, let us see what happens at its end:

> [!bible] [1 Corinthians 15:24-28 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+15:24-28&version=kjv)
> <sup>**24**</sup> Then cometh **the end**, when he shall have delivered up the kingdom to God, even the Father; **when he shall have put down all rule and all authority and power**.
> <sup>**25**</sup> For he must reign, till **he hath put all enemies under his feet**.
> <sup>**26**</sup> **The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death**.
> <sup>**27**</sup> For he hath put all things under his feet. But when he saith, all things are put under him, it is manifest that he is excepted, which did put all things under him.
> <sup>**28**</sup> And when all things shall be subdued unto him, **then shall the Son also himself be subject unto him that put all things under him, that God may be all in all**.

The "*rule and all authority and power*" that Christ shall put down refers to the rebellious angelic powers — Satan and his angels. The Bible uses these very terms to describe them:

> [!bible] [Ephesians 6:12 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+6:12&version=kjv)
> <sup>**12**</sup> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against **principalities**, against **powers**, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

> [!bible] [Romans 8:38-39 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:38-39&version=kjv)
> <sup>**38**</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor **angels**, nor **principalities**, nor **powers**, nor things present, nor things to come,
> <sup>**39**</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

We see that Christ must reign "*till he hath put all enemies under his feet.*" His enemies — Satan and his angels — will **bow down at His feet** and acknowledge His rule and authority. So we see that Satan's rebellion is directed against the reign and authority of Christ.

The final and supreme authority, however, is God the Father. When all things are subdued unto Christ, "*then shall the Son also himself be subject unto him that put all things under him, that God may be all in all.*" The divine order — the Father above all, and the Son subject to the Father — will be fully restored.

We also see that with the defeat of Satan, "*the last enemy that shall be destroyed is death.*" Death itself is a consequence of this great conflict. There is a solution to death, and its destruction signals the complete resolution of the war.

What we can see from this passage is clear: there is a government of God, let by the Son of God, and Satan has rebelled against the authority and the rule of the Son. So what was the reason of this rebellion? What happened to Satan? And who was he in the first place to start this rebellion?
