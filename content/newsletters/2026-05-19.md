Dear friends,

I wanted to share a few updates on what has been happening with The Forgotten Pillar Project. There are new studies to read on notes, and the site itself has gone through some significant changes that I think you will appreciate.

## New Notes

### A Constructive Evaluation of Denis Kaiser's Presentation

Dr. Denis Kaiser presented on the Adventist shift from anti-trinitarianism to trinitarianism. I felt it was important to respond — not with contention, but with the historical record. The Personality of God was the very first article of the Fundamental Principles from 1872 to 1914. The pioneers did not neglect the doctrine of God; it was central to who they were.

Read it here: [Response to Denis Kaiser](https://notes.forgottenpillar.com/response-to-keiser)

### What Is True Education?

If we fail to understand the true science of education, we shall never have a place in the kingdom of God. This note explores what the Bible and Spirit of Prophecy actually mean by education — heaven as a school, Eden as the first classroom, nature as the lesson book, and God Himself as the teacher. How did John the Baptist receive his education, and what does that mean for us today?

Read it here: [What Is True Education?](https://notes.forgottenpillar.com/true-education)

### Hastening the Lord's Return

Do we know when Jesus is coming? And can we do something about it, or are we just passive players watching and waiting? This study looks at what Scripture and the Spirit of Prophecy say about our role in hastening the Second Coming.

Read it here: [Hastening the Lord's Return](https://notes.forgottenpillar.com/hastening-lords-return)

## Major Site Updates

The notes website has received a major update, and I am grateful to share three things with you.

### Push Notifications

You can now subscribe to receive push notifications whenever a new note is published. Click the bell icon at the bottom of any page, allow notifications in your browser, and you are set. You will be notified even when the site is not open. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the bell icon again.

### Install as an App

The site can now be installed as an app on your phone, tablet, or computer. Click the install button at the bottom of the page (or use "Add to Home Screen" on mobile). Once installed, it opens in its own window without browser toolbars — a clean, app-like experience for studying.

### The Notes Are Now Open Source

This has been a long journey, and I am thankful to finally share it: the entire project — the code, the notes, everything — is now open source.

Anyone can contribute. You can write a new note, expand an existing one, fix an error, or suggest improvements. The source code and all notes are hosted on GitHub:

**[github.com/forgotten-pillar/fp-notes](https://github.com/forgotten-pillar/fp-notes)**

If you are interested in contributing, there is a detailed guide that walks you through the process step by step: [Contribution Guide](https://notes.forgottenpillar.com/contribute)

This is a very unique software product — a collaborative, wiki-style Bible study resource built on open-source tools. It has been a labor of love, and I believe the Lord can use many hands to build something that none of us could build alone.

## Stay Connected
