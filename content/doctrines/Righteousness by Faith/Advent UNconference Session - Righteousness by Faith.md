> [!importance] Importance to understand the nature of Christ
> Heb 2:16 For verily he took not on him the nature of angels; but he took on him the seed of Abraham.

> “Humanity Can Keep God's Law by Divine Power— Christ overcame the temptations of Satan as a man. Every man may overcome as Christ overcame. He humbled Himself for us. He was tempted in all points like as we are. He redeemed Adam's disgraceful failure and fall, and was conqueror, thus testifying to all the unfallen worlds and to fallen humanity that man could keep the commandments of God through the divine power granted to him of heaven. Jesus the Son of God humbled Himself for us, endured temptation for us, overcame in our behalf to show us how we may overcome. He has thus bound up His interests with humanity by the closest ties, and has given the positive assurance that we shall not be tempted above that we are able, for with the temptation He will make a way of escape.” Selected Messages (3 SM 136.3)

He came to this world and lived a sinless life, **that in His power His people might also live lives of *sinlessness***. > Those who believe on Christ and obey His commandments are not under bondage to God's law; for to those who believe and obey, His law is not a law of bondage, but of liberty.
