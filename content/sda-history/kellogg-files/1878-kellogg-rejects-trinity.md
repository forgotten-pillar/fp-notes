In 1878, in his book "The Soul and the Resurrection - Harmony of Science and the Bible on the Nature of the Soul and the Doctrine of the Resurrection", J. H. Kellogg made a statement where we can see that he rejects both trinitarian formulation and Athanasian Creed.

> [!quote] The Soul and the Resurrection, p. 32.
> A man whose mind has been expanded by converse with Nature, by the study of the infinite wisdom and goodness of God through his works, **can no more believe in the doctrines of the Athanasian creed** than he can make a world and people it. The doctrine of eternal punishment, as elucidated by Jonathan Edwards, is most offensively repugnant to such a soul.
