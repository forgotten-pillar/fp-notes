The Living Temple contains the alpha of deadly heresies:

> I am instructed to speak plainly. “Meet it,” is the word spoken to me. “Meet it firmly, and without delay.” **But it is not to be met by our taking our working forces from the field to investigate doctrines and points of difference**. We have no such investigation to make. **In the book “Living Temple” there is presented the alpha of deadly heresies**. The omega will follow, and will be received by those who are not willing to heed the warning God has given. [{SpTB02 50.2}](https://legacy.egwwritings.org/?ref=en_SpTB02.50.2&para=417.256)

- The Living Temple contains the alpha of deadly heres**ies** - *plural*
	- People are trying to pinpoint [[The error of the Living Temple]], which would give a need to investigate the doctrines and points of difference. We have no such investigation to take. Rather we should look into what the [[Warning God has given about the Living Temple]] and take heed to that warning
- [[Omega Heresy|Omega]] will follow alpha

> **I have been instructed by *the heavenly messenger*** that *some of the reasoning* in the book, “Living Temple,” is unsound and that **this reasoning would lead astray the minds of those who are not thoroughly established on *the foundation principles of present truth***. It introduces that which is **naught but speculation in regard to *the personality of God and where His presence*** is. No one on this earth has a right to speculate on this question. The more fanciful theories are discussed, the less men will know of God and of the truth that sanctifies the soul. [{SpTB02 51.3}](https://legacy.egwwritings.org/?ref=en_SpTB02.51.3&para=417.262)

- This is pinpointed warning about what the issue with the Living Temple was. This was done by the Heavenly Messenger.
	- unsound reasoning on [[The presence and the personality of God]]
	- leading astray from [[The Foundation Principles of present truth]] - in the context of the chapter we see that those are [[The Fundamental Principles]]


> [!abstract] CONCLUSION
> According to given data, we can give proactive truth driven answer to what constituted the alpha deadly heresies. They were doctrines which lead astray from [[The Foundation Principles of present truth]] or [[The Fundamental Principles]] specifically on the point on the [[The presence and the personality of God]]. So instead of focusing on the error, we should focus on the truth. In this case, this is the doctrine on the [[The presence and the personality of God]] expressed in the [[The Fundamental Principles]]
