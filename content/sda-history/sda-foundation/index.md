title: SDA Foundation
description: The “SDA Foundation” folder explores the Fundamental Principles that form the foundation of Seventh-day Adventist faith, as outlined by the early pioneers and consistently upheld by Ellen G. White throughout her life. It examines the core beliefs that were central to the SDA movement, focusing on their biblical basis and how they shaped the identity of the church. This section also addresses the consequences of departing from the Fundamental Principles, analyzing how new doctrines and practices have been introduced in place of our old foundational truths. It emphasizes Ellen G. White’s warnings about straying from the established teachings and the spiritual implications of such changes. Through scriptural insights and historical reflection, the folder encourages a return to the Fundamental Principles that has made us what we are - the Seventh-day Adventist.
