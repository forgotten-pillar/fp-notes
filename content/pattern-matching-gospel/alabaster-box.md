> [!bible] [Mark 14:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+14:3&version=kjv)
> 3. And being in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he sat at meat, there came a woman having **an alabaster box of ointment of spikenard very precious**; and *she brake the box*, and **poured it on his head**.

> [!bible] [John 12:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+12:3&version=kjv)
> 3. Then took Mary a pound of **ointment of spikenard, very costly**, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and *wiped his feet with her hair*: **and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment**.

> [!bible] [Matthew 26:12 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+26:12&version=kjv)
> 12. For in that she hath **poured this ointment** on my body, she did it for **my burial**.

> Christ told Mary the meaning of her act, and in this He gave her more than He had received. "In that she hath poured this ointment on My body," He said, "she did it for My burial." **As the alabaster box was broken, and filled the whole house with its fragrance, so Christ was to die, His body was to be broken; but He was to rise from the tomb, and the fragrance of His life was to fill the earth**. Christ "hath loved us, and hath given Himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweet-smelling savor." Ephesians 5:2. [{EGW; DA 560.5; 1898}](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p130.2738&index=0)

> [!bible] [Ephesians 5:2 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5:2&version=kjv)
> 2. And walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath **given himself for us** *an offering* and *a sacrifice* to God for **a sweetsmelling savour**.

> [!example] Poem
> In alabaster poured, a prophecy untold,
> Mary's act of love, more precious than gold.  
> Broken like the flask, His body shall be, 
> Yet from death's grasp, He'll rise, set us free.
> 
> Fragrance fills the air, His sacrifice divine,  
> Christ's eternal love, through ages will shine.  
> For us, He gave all, a sweet-smelling savor,  
> His life, a gift, in His grace, we favor.
