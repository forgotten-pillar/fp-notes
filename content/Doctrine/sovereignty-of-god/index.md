title: The Sovereignty of God
description: The “Sovereignty of God” folder focuses on the biblical teaching that God is the supreme ruler over all creation, holding ultimate authority and control over everything in the universe. It explores the nature of God’s sovereignty, His divine will, and how His plans unfold in human history. This section includes an in-depth look at God’s power, wisdom, and authority, emphasizing that He governs the world with justice, love, and mercy. Through scripture and the writings of Ellen G. White, the folder addresses topics such as God’s providence, His governance of free will, and the relationship between divine sovereignty and human responsibility. It also examines the assurance believers have in God’s rule, trust in His plans, and the hope that His sovereign will ultimately leads to the fulfillment of His eternal purpose.
