## Present Truth - a truth specially applicable for their time

> Opposition is the lot of all whom God employs **to present truths specially applicable to their time**. There was a **present truth in the days of Luther**,—a **truth at that time of special importance; there is a present truth for the church today**. He who does all things according to the counsel of his will, has been pleased to place men under various circumstances, and to enjoin upon them duties peculiar to the times in which they live, and the conditions under which they are placed. **If they would prize the light given them, broader views of truth would be opened before them**.
