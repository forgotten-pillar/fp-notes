> The **Great Disappointment** in the [Millerite movement](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Millerite_movement "Millerite movement") was the reaction that followed [Baptist](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Baptist "Baptist") preacher [William Miller](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/William_Miller_(preacher) "William Miller (preacher)")'s proclamation that [Jesus Christ](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Jesus_Christ "Jesus Christ") would return to the Earth by 1844, which he called the [Second Advent](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Second_Coming "Second Coming"). His study of the [Daniel 8](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Daniel_8 "Daniel 8") prophecy during the [Second Great Awakening](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Second_Great_Awakening "Second Great Awakening") led him to conclude that Daniel's "cleansing of the sanctuary" was cleansing the world from sin when Christ would come, and he and many others prepared. When Jesus did not appear by October 22, 1844, Miller and his followers were disappointed.[](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Great_Disappointment#cite_note-news.adventist.org-1)[](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Great_Disappointment#cite_note-pbs.org-2)[](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Great_Disappointment#cite_note-amazingdiscoveries.org-3)[](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Great_Disappointment#cite_note-adventistreview.org-4)
> 
> These events paved the way for the [Adventists](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Adventism "Adventism") who formed the [Seventh-day Adventist Church](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Seventh-day_Adventist_Church "Seventh-day Adventist Church"). They contended that what had happened on October 22 was not Jesus's return, as Miller had thought, but the start of Jesus's final work of atonement, the cleansing in the [heavenly sanctuary](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Heavenly_sanctuary "Heavenly sanctuary"), leading up to the [Second Coming](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Second_Coming "Second Coming").
