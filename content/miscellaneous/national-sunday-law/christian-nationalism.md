> [!bible] [Luke 17:20-24 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+17:20-24&version=kjv)
> <sup>**20**</sup> And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come, he answered them and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation:
> <sup>**21**</sup> Neither shall they say, Lo here! or, lo there! for, behold, the kingdom of God is within you.
> <sup>**22**</sup> And he said unto the disciples, The days will come, when ye shall desire to see one of the days of the Son of man, and ye shall not seeit.
> <sup>**23**</sup> And they shall say to you, See here; or, see there: go not after them, nor follow them.
> <sup>**24**</sup> For as the lightning, that lighteneth out of the one part  under heaven, shineth unto the other part under heaven; so shall also the Son of man be in his day.

Verse 23 is about Christian Nationalism. It is warning against the false Christ which will come in observable fashion right before Christ's second coming. The Kingdom of God will observably come in the Second Coming of Christ, when He comes visibly as the lightning that lightenet out of the one part under heaven and shineth unto the other part under heaven. But prior to His coming, the kingdom of God does not come with observation, because it is within you. It is inward working principle which needs to be established first within the heart. The heart needs to be transformed, the culture of the Kingdom of God needs to be established and fully rooter in its citizens. The laws of the Kingdom of God needs to be cultivated, from the right motives - the love towards the King and the Law Giver. 

But just as Jesus prophesied, "they shall say to you, See here; or, see there: go not after them, nor follow them". Just as Pharisees which were Jewish nationalists, desired the kingdom of God to be visible kingdom as representing of God's rule on earth, Jesus pointed to two things: the true nature of His Kingdom, and raise of religious nationalism just prior of His Second coming.
