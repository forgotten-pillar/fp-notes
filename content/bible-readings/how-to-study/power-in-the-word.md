title: Power in the Word
---
1. Through what agency did God create the heavens?

> [!bible] [Psalms 33:6-9 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+33:6-9&version=kjv)
> <sup>**6**</sup> **By the word of the LORD were the heavens made**; and all the host of them **by the breath of his mouth**.
> <sup>**9**</sup> **For he spake**, and it was done; he commanded, and it stood fast.

***
2. By what does Christ uphold all things?

> [!bible] [Hebrews 1:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+1:3&version=kjv)
> <sup>**3**</sup> ...upholding all things **by the word of his power**, ...;

***
3. Of what are some willingly ignorant?

> [!bible] [2 Peter 3:5-6 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Peter+3:5-6&version=kjv)
> <sup>**5**</sup> For this they willingly are ignorant of, that **by the word of God the heavens were of old**, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water:
> <sup>**6**</sup> **Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished**:

***
4. By what are the present heavens and earth for a similar fate?

> [!bible] [2 Peter 3:7 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Peter+3:7&version=kjv)
> <sup>**7**</sup> But the heavens and the earth, which are now, **by the same word** are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.


***
5. In what other scripture is it shown that creative power is exercised through the word of God?

> [!bible] [Psalms 148:5 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+148:5&version=kjv)
> <sup>**5**</sup> Let them praise the name of the LORD: **for he commanded, and they were created**.

***
6. What change is wrought in one who is in Christ?

> [!bible] [2 Corinthians 5:17 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+5:17&version=kjv)
> <sup>**17**</sup> Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is **a new creature** [there is a new creation, R .V., margin]: old things are passed away; behold, **all things are become new**.

***
7. What is this new creation also called?

> [!bible] [John 3:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3:3&version=kjv)
> <sup>**3**</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be **born again**, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

***
8. Through what agency is this new creation, or new birth accomplished?

> [!bible] [1 Peter 1:23 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Peter+1:23&version=kjv)
> <sup>**23**</sup> Being **born again**, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, **by the word of God**, which liveth and abideth for ever.

***
9. What is the first creative commandment recorded in the Bible? And what was the result of it?

> [!bible] [Genesis 1:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+1:3&version=kjv)
> <sup>**3**</sup> And God said, **Let there be light**: and **there was light**.

***
10. What connection is there between the creation of light in the beginning, and the light of the gospel?

> [!bible] [2 Corinthians 4:6 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+4:6&version=kjv)
> <sup>**6**</sup> For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, **to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ**.

***
11. Why were the people astonished at Christ's teaching?

> [!bible] [Luke 4:32 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+4:32&version=kjv)
> <sup>**32**</sup> And they were astonished at his doctrine: **for his word was with power**.

***
12. What testified to the power of the word of Christ?

> [!bible] [Luke 4:36 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+4:36&version=kjv)
> <sup>**36**</sup> And they were all amazed, and spake among themselves, saying, What a word is this! for **with authority and power he commandeth the unclean spirits, and they come out**.

***
13. What did Christ say is the seed of the kingdom of God?

> [!bible] [Luke 8:11 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+8:11&version=kjv)
> <sup>**11**</sup> Now the parable is this: **The seed is the word of God**.

***
14. Where should the word of Christ dwell?

> [!bible] [Colossians 3:16 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians+3:16&version=kjv)
> <sup>**16**</sup> Let the word of Christ **dwell in you** richly in all wisdom; ....

***
15. What did Christ say of the unbelieving Jews respecting the word of God?

> [!bible] [John 5:38 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+5:38&version=kjv)
> <sup>**38**</sup> **And ye have not His word abiding in you**: for whom he hath sent, him ye believe not.

***
16. How does the word of God work in the believer?

> [!bible] [1 Thessalonians 2:13 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Thessalonians+2:13&version=kjv)
> <sup>**13**</sup> For this cause also thank we God without ceasing, because, when ye received the word of God which ye heard of us, ye received it not as the word of men, but as it is in truth, the word of God, **which effectually worketh also in you that believe**.

***
17. What nature is imparted through the promise of God?

> [!bible] [2 Peter 1:4 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Peter+1:4&version=kjv)
> <sup>**4**</sup> Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that **by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature**, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.

***
18. By what are believers made clean?

> [!bible] [John 15:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+15:3&version=kjv)
> <sup>**3**</sup> Now ye are clean **through the word** which I have spoken unto you.

***
19. How may a young man cleanse his way?

> [!bible] [Psalms 119:9 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+119:9&version=kjv)
> <sup>**9**</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? **by taking heed thereto  according to thy word**.

***
20. How did God heal His people anciently?

> [!bible] [Psalms 107:20 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+107:20&version=kjv)
> <sup>**20**</sup> **He sent his word, and healed them**, and delivered them from their destructions.

***
21. How did the centurion show his faith in the power of Christ's word to heal?

> [!bible] [Matthew 8:8 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+8:8&version=kjv)
> <sup>**8**</sup> The centurion answered and said, Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but **speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed**.

***
22. What power has the word when hidden in the heart?

> [!bible] [Psalms 119:11 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+119:11&version=kjv)
> <sup>**11**</sup> Thy word have I hid in mine heart, **that I might not sin against thee**.

See also [Psalm 17:4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+17.4&version=KJV)

***
23. Why did God humble Israel, and suffer them to hunger?

> [!bible] [Deuteronomy 8:3 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+8:3&version=kjv)
> <sup>**3**</sup> And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, ... **that He might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word  that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live**.

***
24. What is required beyond a mere hearing of the word?

> [!bible] [James 1:22 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1:22&version=kjv)
> <sup>**22**</sup> **But be ye doers of the word**, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.

***
25. What is the result of doing God's will?

> [!bible] [1 John 2:17 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+2:17&version=kjv)
> <sup>**17**</sup> And the world passeth away, and the lust thereof: but **he that doeth the will of God abideth for ever**.
