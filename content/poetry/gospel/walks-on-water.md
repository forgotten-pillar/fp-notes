Based upon: [Matthew 14:22-33](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+14.22-33&version=KJV)

In storms, we're Peter on the sea,  
Eyes fixed on waves, not on Thee.  
Yet, You call us by our name,  
Through waters deep, Your promise remains.  
"Fear not," You say, "for you are Mine,  
Through fire and flood, I'm by your side."  
The Lord, our God, our Holy Guide,  
In His grace, we shall abide.
