> [!bible]- [John 10:14-16 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+10:14-16&version=kjv)
> 14. I am the good shepherd, and know my sheep, and am known of mine.
> 15. As the Father knoweth me, even so know I the Father: and I lay down my life for the sheep.
> 16. And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and  one shepherd.

> [!bible]- [Ezekiel 34:31 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ezekiel+34:31&version=kjv)
> 31. And ye my flock, the flock of my pasture, are men, and  I am your God, saith the Lord GOD.

> [!bible]- [Isaiah 43:1 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+43:1&version=kjv)
> 1. But now thus saith the LORD that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.

> [!bible]- [Isaiah 49:16 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+49:16&version=kjv)
> 16. Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.

Over hills and through the woods,
Shepherd guides with care so good.
Each name known, in heart so deep,
Through lonely nights, their watch he keeps.

Tender bonds that never sleep,
His flock, his own, forever keep.
As divine Shepherd knows His own,
We're called by name, never alone.
