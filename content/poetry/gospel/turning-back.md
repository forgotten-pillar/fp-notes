Summary of [John 6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+6&version=KJV)

In crowds, they cheer, with bread, they're fed,
Triumph's echo, joy widespread.

But when truth's light their sins request,
To leave behind, for higher quest,

Many turn, their backs now shown,
Leaving Jesus to walk alone.
