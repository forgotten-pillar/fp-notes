> [!bible] [Matthew 10:28 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+10:28&version=kjv)
> <sup> **28** </sup>And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him **which is able to destroy** both **soul** and body **in hell**.

> [!bible] [Ezekiel 18:4 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ezekiel+18:4&version=kjv)
> <sup> **4** </sup>Behold, **all souls are mine**; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is mine: **the soul that sinneth, it shall die**.

Duša umire jer je sagriješila, i svaka duša koja zgriješi, umrijeti će. A kako će živjeti? Odgovor je u evanđelju, u onome što mojem narodu fali:

> [!bible] [John 3:16 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3:16&version=kjv)
> <sup> **16** </sup>For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever **believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life**.

O kakvom propadanju života Isus ovdje govori? Govori o svojoj žrtvi koju je On platio za moj grijeh, jer zgriješih i svakako će mi duša umrijeti - život će propasti, no prihvatim li Njegovu smrt, i Njegov život, živjeti ću vječno. No duša bez Isusa je smrta, jer je sagriješila.
