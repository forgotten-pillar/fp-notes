> [!bible]+ [Leviticus 16:21 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+16:21&version=kjv)
> 21. And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions in all their sins, **putting them upon the head of the goat**, and shall send him away by the hand of ***a fit man*** into **the wilderness**:

Fit man is the person who leads away the scapegoat to the wilderness, after the High Priest puts on him all the sins of Israel. This man has an important role in finalizing the atonement of Israel.

> **Not until the goat had been thus sent away did the people regard themselves as freed from the burden of their sins**. [PP 355.4](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p84.1596&index=0)

> [!important] Notice
> The problem of sin is not resolved when Christ stops the intercession, but it is resolved when the scapegoat is in the wilderness. This work has been placed upon the fit man.

> [!info]+ Pattern Matching
> The earthly sanctuary was the type of the heavenly sanctuary. Earthly sanctuary service was a type or an object lesson for the Plan of Redemption. See [[The Sanctuary Service]]

The scapegoat is representing Satan:

> Since Satan is the originator of sin, the direct instigator of all the sins that caused the death of the Son of God, justice demands that Satan shall suffer the final punishment. **Christ's work for the redemption of men and the purification of the universe from sin will be closed by the removal of sin from the heavenly sanctuary and the placing of these sins upon Satan**, who will bear the final penalty. So in the typical service, the yearly round of ministration closed with the purification of the sanctuary, **and the confessing of the sins on the head of the scapegoat**. [PP 358.2](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p84.1611&index=0)
## Who is the *fit man*?

> The scapegoat came no more into the camp of Israel, **and the man who led him away was required to wash himself and his clothing with water before returning to the camp**. [GC 419.2](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p132.1901&index=0)

The purification of the fit man highlights the need for us to be *spiritually pure* to fulfill this role:

> Those who are living upon the earth when the intercession of Christ shall cease in the sanctuary above, are **to stand in the sight of a holy God without a mediator**. **Their robes must be spotless, their characters must be purified from sin by the blood of sprinkling**. Through the grace of God and their **own diligent effort**, they must be conquerors in the battle with evil. [GC88 425.1](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p133.1938&index=0) #victoryOverSin #finalGeneration

> Leading away the scapegoat into the wilderness is after the mediation of Christ, yet this man has to be blameless. This is man pattern matches with #144k in the final generation ([Revelation 14:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+14.5&version=KJV))

> Satan could find nothing in the Son of God that would enable him to gain the victory. He had kept his Father's commandments, and there was no sin in him that Satan could use to his advantage. **This is the condition in which those must be found who shall stand in the time of trouble**. ## Importance of the fit man role

> Then I saw that Jesus’ work in the sanctuary was almost finished, almost finished, and after His work there is finished He will come to the door of the tabernacle, or door of the first apartment, **and confess the sins of Israel upon the head of the scape goat**. Then He will put on the garments of vengeance. Then the plagues come upon the wicked, and they do not come until Jesus puts on the garments of vengeance and takes His seat upon the great white cloud. **Then while the plagues are falling the scape goat is being led away**. **He makes a mighty struggle to escape, but *he is held fast by the hand that bears him away***. *If he should effect his escape Israel would be destroyed (or slain)*. I saw that it would take time to bear him away into the land of forgetfulness after the sins were put upon his head. [1LtMs, Ms 15, 1850, par. 8](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p13961.2893013&index=0)

> [!important] The final step to the redemption
> Every step of the Plan of Salvation needs to be completed. The last step is leading the scapegoat out into the wilderness. But this leading is not done by Christ, but by the *fit man*. What would happen if the scapegoat escapes?

Should the scapegoat escape, not only would the 144,000 fail, but all the saved would lose their lives. The plan of redemption would be for nothing. This is why Satan tries so hard to get us to [[battle-for-our-time|lose our focus]], forget our identity, and remain asleep.

God is risking everything on the success of the 144,000. We cannot lose track of our calling as fit men to drag the devil into the wilderness. We cannot let the scapegoat escape; if it does, the Plan of Salvation would be proven insufficient, and Satan’s accusations that the law cannot be kept would be justified.

> [!quote] I saw that it would take time to bear him away
> This is little time of trouble, when we should stand before God without an intercesor

> [!quote] the land of forgefulness
> The land of forgefulness where the scapegoat is led away is the bounding Satan in [Rev 20:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rev+20.2&version=KJV) for 1000 years, in this waste land on this planet
