> [!bible] [Revelation 14:7 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+14:7&version=kjv)
> 7. Saying with a loud voice, **Fear God**, and **give glory to him**; for **the hour of his judgment is come**: and **worship him** that *made* heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters.

Who is God from the first Angel's Message? Who is the God we ought to worship and give glory to?

This question is important since [Revelation 13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+13&version=KJV) reveals that the mankind will be on the test in regards to God we are worshiping.

Revelation 14:7 is giving us several clues:
- Contextually, He is God described in book of Revelation
- He is God whose judgment has come
- He is the Creator

Let's explore all three clues.

## "His Judgment has Come"

This judgment is timely set. If we can know which judgment is spoken of in the First Angel's Message, it will be easier for us to understand whose judgment is it? Because in the Scriptures we have multiple judgments. This one particular is referring to the investigative judgment which commenced in 1844. This was prophesied in [Daniel 8:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Daniel+8.14&version=KJV):

> [!bible] [Daniel 8:14 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Daniel+8:14&version=kjv)
> 14. And he said unto me, **Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall *the sanctuary be cleansed***.


## "Who Created..."

The book of Revelation gives us the answer who is God the Creator:

> [!bible] [Revelation 10:6 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+10:6&version=kjv)
> 6. [the angel with an open book] And sware by ***Him that liveth for ever and ever*, who created heaven, and the things that therein are, and the earth, and the things that therein are, and the sea, and the things which are therein**, that there should be time no longer:

In the book of Revelation, the God who created everything is depicted as God **who liveth for ever and ever**. The book of Revelation commonly attach this expression to God. Who is He?

> [!bible] [Revelation 4:10-11 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+4:10-11&version=kjv)
> 10. The four and twenty elders fall down **before him that sat on the throne**, and **worship him that liveth for ever and ever**, and cast their crowns before the throne, saying,
> 11. Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: **for thou hast created all things**, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.

By no mistake, the twenty elders worship God the Creator from the First Angel's Message. He is God who liveth for ever and ever, the God **who sits on the throne**.

In Revelation 5:13,14 we can identify clearly Who is this God:

> [!bible] [Revelation 5:13-14 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5:13-14&version=kjv)
> 13. And every creature which is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and such as are in the sea, and all that are in them [all created beings, created by God the Creator], heard I saying, Blessing, and honour, and **glory**, and power, be **unto him that sitteth upon the throne**, and unto *the Lamb for ever and ever*.
> 14. And the four beasts said, Amen. And the four and twenty elders fell down and **worshipped him that liveth for ever and ever**.

The Lamb in the text is Jesus ([John 1:29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1.29&version=KJV), [36](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1.36&version=KJV)). And the One who is depicted as God who sits on the throne, who liveth for ever and ever, who is God the Creator is God **the Father**. This is clearly seen in the context of [chapter 5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5&version=KJV). See verses [1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5.1&version=KJV), [6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5.6&version=KJV) and [7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5.7&version=KJV). Jesus - the Lamb, is a distinct person from God in book Revelation. And in continuation of [chapter 5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5&version=KJV), we see twenty four elders and 4 beast giving glory to the Father and the Son - to God and the Lamb. Pay close attention to Who is God for these 24 elders?

> [!bible] [Revelation 5:8-13 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+5:8-13&version=kjv)
> 8. And when he had taken the book, the four beasts and four and  twenty elders **fell down before the Lamb**, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints.
> 9. And they sung a new song, saying, **Thou art worthy** to take the book, and to open the seals thereof: for thou wast slain, and **hast redeemed us to God** by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation;
> 10. And hast made us unto **our God** kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth.
> 11. And I beheld, and I heard the voice of many angels round about the throne and the beasts and the elders: and the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands of thousands;
> 12. Saying with a loud voice, **Worthy is the Lamb** that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.
> 13. And every creature which is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and such as are in the sea, and all that are in them, heard I saying, **Blessing, and honour, and glory, and power, be unto *him that sitteth upon the throne*, and unto the Lamb for ever and ever**.

The person whom these twenty four elders deem as God, and to Whom they address as their God is the Father. But also they are giving the glory to His Son.
