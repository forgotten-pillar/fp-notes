
> [!question] Is the doctrine on the personality of God mystery?
> Is it unknown? If so how could something unknown, and mysterious be part of our foundation, and distinct enough to recognize when someone is departing from it?

## Part of our foundation

> Some are to depart from the faith. Where are they? Who are they? Who is departing from the faith laid down, **the very foundation that we were on a whole century ago**? We are on the **very same foundation**; we have the same evidence, and **we worked on it day and night**, to know in regard to the sanctuary question, **and in regard to the personality of God, and of Christ, and of all these subjects**. [21LtMs, Ms38, 1906](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p14071.9662048&index=0)

See also [[fundamental-principles|The Fundamental Principles]] and [[adventist-pioneers-on-trinity|Adventist Pioneers on the Trinity]].

> [!important] The personality of God part of our SDA foundation
> In particular Ellen White classed the personality of God, and of Christ in the foundation of our faith. And we should not depart from it. It has equal weight as the Sanctuary doctrine

## Was the pillar of our faith

> Those who seek to remove **the old landmarks** are not holding fast; they are not remembering how they have received and heard. Those who try to bring in theories that would remove **the pillars of our faith** concerning the sanctuary, or **concerning the personality of God or of Christ**, are working as blind men. They are seeking to bring in uncertainties and to set the people of God adrift, without an anchor. [20LtMs, Ms 62, 1905](https://egwwritings.org/read?panels=p14070.10026020&index=0)

> [!important] The personality of God - landmark, the pillar
> In particular Ellen White classed the personality of God, and of Christ as the pillar or landmark of our faith. She have put its importance on the side with the Sanctuary doctrine
