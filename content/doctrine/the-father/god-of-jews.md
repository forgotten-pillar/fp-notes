Who is God of the Jews? All records from the Scriptures points to God the Father. Let's start with Jesus' own testimony. As a Jew He said to the Samaritan woman:

> [!bible] [John 4:22-23 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+4:22-23&version=kjv)
> 22. Ye worship *ye know not what*: **we** [Jews] **know what we worship**: for salvation is of the Jews.
> 23. But the hour cometh, and now is, when **the true worshippers shall worship the Father** in spirit and in truth: for the Father seeketh such to worship him.

There is no mistake here. Jews knew what and who they worship. It is the Father. Their understanding of who God was, is correct understanding according to Christ's own testimony.

His disciples were Jews, and He said to them:

> [!bible] [John 14:1 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+14:1&version=kjv)
> 1. Let not your heart be troubled: **ye believe in God, believe also in me**.

Jesus pointed to the Father.

## God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob - the Father

> [!bible] [Acts 3:1,22 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+3:13-22&version=kjv)
> 13. **The God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, *the God of our fathers***, hath glorified **his Son Jesus**; whom ye delivered up, and denied him in the presence of Pilate, when he was determined to let him go.
> 22. For Moses truly said *unto the fathers*, A **prophet** [that's Jesus] shall **the Lord your God** raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear in all things whatsoever he shall say unto you.
