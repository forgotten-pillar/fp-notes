Abrahamic religions are monotheistic religions. Judaism, Islam and Christianity. Christian view of monotheism is distinct from the Judaism and Islam. We are interested on what does the Bible say about monotheism. The Old Testament holds its authority for Jews, while the New Testament, in addition to Old, hold authority to Christians. Therefore let us examine the Biblical testimony to the monotheism. Let's examine all Bible verses which are talking about one God.

## Shema

> [!bible] [Deuteronomy 6:4 - KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+6:4&version=kjv)
> <sup> **4** </sup>Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is **one LORD**:

[Shema](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Shema) is the foundational statement of Jewish faith. Jesus' agreed with that standpoint. At one point, a scribe came to Him, and asked Him which is the first commandment of all. Jesus answered with "*Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is **one LORD***". Contrary to the popular contemporary Christian belief that one LORD is [a unity of three persons](https://www.adventist.org/trinity/), this scribe acknowledged Christ response "Well, Master, **thou hast said the truth: for there is one God; and there is *none other but he***". Scribe's acknowledgment of the fact that there is none other but He, as well supreme devotion of love to Him, was a discreet answer, which Jesus sanctioned, acknowledge that Jewish understanding of monotheism proclaimed in [Deuteronomy 6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy+6&version=KJV) is the correct one. Jesus Himself interpreted Deuteronomy 6 as a reference to His Father - see [[shema-israel|Shema Israel]].
