2. **"To receive from a source by a regular conveyance"**:
   - This means to get something from a particular origin or starting point in a steady and reliable way.

Now, let's think about "continual dependence":

- **Continual** means happening without interruption, ongoing.
- **Dependence** means needing something or someone for support or help.

- The definition suggests that you are regularly getting something from a specific source.
- Because you are getting it regularly and reliably, you are depending on that source to keep providing it.
