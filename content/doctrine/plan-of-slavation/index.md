title: The Plan of Salvation
description: The “Plan of Salvation” folder explores the fundamental biblical doctrine of God’s redemptive work for humanity. It delves into the steps God has taken to restore fallen humanity to a state of grace, highlighting key themes such as creation, the fall, the promise of a Savior, the life and sacrifice of Jesus Christ, and the ultimate hope of eternal life. This section examines scripture and insights from the Spirit of Prophecy, including Ellen G. White’s writings, to provide a comprehensive understanding of God’s plan. Topics include justification, sanctification, and glorification, along with the role of faith, repentance, and the work of the Holy Spirit in salvation. This collection serves as a spiritual guide for understanding God’s love and purpose in offering salvation to all.
